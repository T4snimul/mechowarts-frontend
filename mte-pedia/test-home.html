About the platform

Built by the department, for every batch that follows.

MTEPedia is the academic resource platform for the Department of Mechatronics Engineering at Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET). It gathers semester-wise study material into one place students can actually navigate — instead of scattered drive links and forwarded messages.

Lecture notes, previous examination questions, laboratory manuals, assignments and departmental notices are organized so any student, from first semester to final year, can find what they need in a few clicks.

The goal is simple: learn, collaborate, innovate — and build a resource that gets better with every batch that contributes to it.
